# Design and UX patterns worth borrowing

Each pattern was seen on a live site on 12 October 2026 and is tied to a screenshot in `screenshots/`. The "For Aspley" line says how it would apply to a pub with a bistro, bars, gaming, functions, a bottle shop and Star Rewards.

These are inputs to the design phase, not decisions. The installed design skills (`impeccable`, `frontend-design`, `ui-ux-pro-max`) decide the final direction, and their anti-template rules apply. Several patterns below are exactly what the ALH and AVC template sites share, so copying them literally would make Aspley look like one more template. Borrow the job each pattern does, not its look.

## Getting the guest to book

1. **One "Book a table" button, always visible in the header.**
   Seen on Everton Park, Taigum and the Normanby (`brisbane-02-everton-park-hotel-home-desktop-fold.jpg`, `pub-benchmark-03-normanby-hotel-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
   For Aspley: replace the header "CALL" button with "Book a table", and keep tap-to-call as a secondary icon on mobile.

2. **A single "Book" menu that lists every way to book.**
   Seen on Desa Potato Head, where one button opens Hotel Reservation, Beach Club Daybed, Restaurants, Event Tickets and Shop (`benchmark-05-potato-head-seminyak-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
   For Aspley: one menu with Book a table, Plan a function, Buy event tickets and Join Star Rewards.

3. **"Would you like to…" action list beside the welcome copy.**
   Seen on Everton Park, Taigum and the Regatta: Book a table, View the menu, Live sport, Buy a gift voucher (`brisbane-10-taigum-tavern-home-desktop.jpg`, `pub-benchmark-01-regatta-hotel-home-desktop.jpg`).
   For Aspley: the four or five things locals actually come to do, as large tap targets.

4. **Separate Book buttons for each bookable area.**
   Seen on Breakfast Creek ("Book Beer Garden", "Book Spanish Garden") and the Regatta ("Book the Boatshed") (`pub-benchmark-02-breakfast-creek-hotel-booking-desktop.jpg`).
   For Aspley: bistro and function areas each get their own booking path once the venue confirms which areas take bookings.

5. **Two primary actions on the hero, nothing more.**
   Seen on The Prince ("Book a room", "View gig guide") and the Regatta ("Book the Boatshed", "Host a function") (`benchmark-04-the-prince-st-kilda-home-desktop-fold.jpg`, `pub-benchmark-01-regatta-hotel-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
   For Aspley: "Book a table" and "What's on this week".

6. **Slim announcement bar above the navigation with one action.**
   Seen on Albany Creek, Kedron Park and Breakfast Creek ("Christmas Day bookings are now open! Book Now") (`brisbane-09-albany-creek-tavern-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
   For Aspley: Melbourne Cup, Christmas Day or a jackpot draw, editable by venue staff and dismissible.

7a. **A sticky "Book now / View menu" bar at the bottom of the phone screen.**
   Seen on Albany Creek, Kedron Park and Breakfast Creek (`overview-pubs-first-screen-mobile.jpg`, `brisbane-03-kedron-park-hotel-home-mobile-fold.jpg`).
   For Aspley: the two actions guests want most stay under the thumb on every page.

## Events and specials

7. **"What's on today" first, then "More events and specials" as a list.**
   Seen on Everton Park and the Regatta (`brisbane-02-everton-park-hotel-home-desktop.jpg`).
   For Aspley: today's offer up top, so a guest on a Wednesday sees Trivia and the parmy deal without scrolling a carousel.

8. **Event cards with a date, a line of copy and their own button.**
   Seen on Albany Creek and Kedron Park, where each card has "Book now" or "Find out more" (`brisbane-09-albany-creek-tavern-home-desktop.jpg`).
   For Aspley: replace poster images with real cards, so Google can read event names and dates and the posters can still appear as artwork.

9. **Weekly specials with the price on the card.**
   Seen on Taigum ("$16 lunches", "Wednesday parmy") (`brisbane-10-taigum-tavern-functions-desktop.jpg`).
   For Aspley: show members pricing beside public pricing, which is the strongest reason to join Star Rewards.

10. **Ticketed events linked to a working checkout.**
    Seen on the Normanby, where event posters carry "Click for tickets" through to a ticketing provider (`pub-benchmark-03-normanby-hotel-home-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: the current Melbourne Cup checkout is broken. Any working ticket path is an immediate win.

## Functions

11. **A function-space finder with filters.**
    Seen on the Regatta: ten spaces filterable by guest count, seated or standing, privacy and event type, each with a photo and capacities (`pub-benchmark-01-regatta-hotel-functions-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: even with fewer spaces, a card per space with capacities and photos beats a PDF package.

12. **A page per occasion.**
    Seen on the Normanby (21st, 30th, 40th, 50th birthdays, engagement, corporate, Christmas, wakes) and the Regatta (birthdays, corporate, engagement, private dining, weddings) (`pub-benchmark-03-normanby-hotel-functions-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: each occasion page ranks in Google for "21st venue Aspley" and similar searches.

13. **"Function pack" and "Enquire now" as two equal buttons.**
    Seen on Albany Creek (`brisbane-09-albany-creek-tavern-functions-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: the pack for people still deciding, the form for people ready to ask.

## Food and drink

14. **Plated food as the hero image.**
    Seen on Albany Creek, Taigum and the Normanby (`overview-pubs-first-screen-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: the site has no food photography today. A half-day shoot is the single biggest visual upgrade.

15. **Menus as real text with prices.**
    Seen on the Normanby, which publishes its menu with prices in the page text.
    For Aspley: dishes, prices and members prices as HTML, with filters for kids, gluten-free and specials. This also fixes the menu pages that never load on phones.

## Brand and trust

16. **Heritage as the headline.**
    Seen on the Normanby ("A Brisbane legend since 1890") and the Regatta ("Iconic riverside pub since 1874") (`pub-benchmark-03-normanby-hotel-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
    For Aspley: "The pub on the hill since 1935" already exists on the badge. Make it the first thing people read.

17. **The building at night as the hero.**
    Seen on Breakfast Creek, where the lit-up facade carries the whole brand (`pub-benchmark-02-breakfast-creek-hotel-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
    For Aspley: the Queenslander facade on the hill is distinctive. A dusk photo would do the same job.

18. **Photo tiles as the main navigation.**
    Seen on the Normanby: Eat, Drink, What's On, Celebrate (`pub-benchmark-03-normanby-hotel-home-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: Eat, Drink, Gaming, Functions, Sports Bar, Bottle Shop as six photo tiles.

19. **"Spaces designed to match every vibe" row.**
    Seen on the Normanby: Beer Garden, Public Bar, Bistro, Gaming Lounge, each as a photo card (`pub-benchmark-03-normanby-hotel-home-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: helps first-time visitors picture the venue.

20. **A real review and a link to leave one.**
    Seen on the Normanby ("One of the best pubs in Brisbane", "Leave your review").
    For Aspley: 4.2 stars from 791 Google reviews is an asset no direct rival is using.

21. **Instagram strip near the footer.**
    Seen on the Normanby (`pub-benchmark-03-normanby-hotel-home-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: shows the venue is alive this week without staff updating the site.

22. **Opening hours per area in one clear block.**
    Seen on Breakfast Creek, which lists general, pizza, Spanish Garden, bistro and late-bar hours separately in the footer (`pub-benchmark-02-breakfast-creek-hotel-booking-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: one source of truth for bistro, bar, gaming and bottle shop hours, also fed into Google structured data. This fixes the contradictory hours found in the audit.

23. **Responsible gambling statement with the helpline.**
    Seen on Taigum, which shows Gambling Help 1800 858 858 in the gaming section (`brisbane-10-taigum-tavern-home-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: required good practice for a venue promoting a 10am to 4am gaming room.

24. **Acknowledgement of Country and an inclusion statement in the footer.**
    Seen on every Australian Venue Co site (`brisbane-02-everton-park-hotel-home-desktop.jpg`).
    For Aspley: a small, expected signal for a community venue.

## Visual craft from the premium benchmarks

25. **Editorial serif headline with a clean sans body.**
    Seen on the Regatta (Playfair Display with Sofia Pro), Ace Hotel (Tiempos Headline) and the Calile (`benchmark-03-ace-hotel-sydney-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
    For Aspley: a confident display face would separate it from the Brandon Grotesque and PT Sans Narrow shared by every ALH site. Final choice comes from the design skills.

26. **Restraint: one hero image, generous space, few words.**
    Seen on the Calile and Halcyon House (`benchmark-01-the-calile-hotel-home-desktop-fold.jpg`, `benchmark-06-halcyon-house-home-desktop-fold.jpg`).
    For Aspley: the current home page stacks nine full-width sections of equal weight. Fewer, stronger sections will read as more premium.

27. **A playful brand moment.**
    Seen on the Calile, which has a "Day mode / Night mode" toggle on its home page (`benchmark-01-the-calile-hotel-home-mobile-fold.jpg`).
    For Aspley: a pub that serves lunch to families and runs gaming until 4am has a genuine day and night personality to play with.
