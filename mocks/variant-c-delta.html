On theHillSince1935

A northside local for more than ninety years.

The white two-storey Queenslander above Gympie Road has been the local since 1935. It joined the Star Hotels family in November 2023. The verandah, the stone steps and the round sign out the front are all still here.

A day at the Aspley

From the first lunch order to the last drive-through run, here's how a day on the hill goes.

Bistro: lunch Sun to Wed 11:30am to 2:30pm and dinner 5:30pm to 8:30pm. Thu to Sat it runs all day, 11:30am to 8:30pm.

  1. 11:30am

    Lunch in the bistro

    Steaks from the grill, the parmy army, burgers and pub classics. Kids meals are $13.95 with a soft drink and ice cream. Star Rewards members pay $1 less on every dish.

  2. 3:00pm

    Arvo on the deck

    Great Northern, Stone & Wood and XXXX Gold on tap, cocktails off the chalkboard and wine by the glass. Coffee and cake is $13.95, or $12.95 for members.

  3. 6:30pm

    Game night

    Dinner from 5:30pm, then Trivia every Wednesday at 6:30pm, free and hosted by Questionable Company. The sports bar screens are on all day.

  4. 10:00pm

    Late

    The gaming room is open every day until 4am. The drive-through bottle shop runs until 11pm, and midnight on Friday and Saturday.
