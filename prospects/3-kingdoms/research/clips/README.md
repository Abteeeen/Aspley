### What works

- It is one unbroken walk from the mall to the counter. That is exactly the "hard to find" answer the research asks for.
- The 4 s shopfront frame (sign, lanterns, red wall, lattice) is the strongest single image. It is sharp and well composed, and makes a good poster frame.
- The lanterns and red walls are a real visual signature no rival in Uptown has.
- A centre crop to 9:16 holds up at 9, 14 and 20 s (lanterns, room, neon sign). See `phone-crop-test.jpg`. Only the opening slat shot fails as a crop.

### What is wrong with it

Close-ups of two of these are in `defects-13s-smear-21s-soft.jpg`.

1. **Motion smear at 12–16 s.** The lanterns ghost into streaks. This comes from a slow shutter plus stabilisation, and it is baked into the pixels. No grade fixes it.
2. **Clipped reds at 12–18 s.** The orange grade pushes the walls to the edge of the 8-bit range. Re-grading this export will band. Re-grade from the camera original instead.
3. **Exposure jumps.** Average brightness roughly doubles between 9 s and 20 s (luma ~56 → ~112). It reads as the camera adjusting, not as a choice.
4. **The ending is soft.** At 21–22 s the menu text is unreadable and the neon is blown out. It is too soft to be the payoff shot.
5. **Real people.** Diners and two staff members are identifiable. Using them on the website needs their consent, or they need to be removed, blurred or reshot.
6. **Off-message details.** The Hungry Panda sticker sells the marketplace the site is meant to replace (pickup at in-store prices). The taped paper notices look untidy at hero size.
7. **Too heavy to use whole.** At 1080p the full clip is 7.2 MB as a web encode. A scroll-scrub-ready encode (frequent keyframes) is 6.4–12 MB even at 720p. Shortened to an 8 s loop it is 2.5 MB in H.264 and 1.3 MB in AV1. Encode tests were run on 13 Oct 2026.

### Which concept it suits

- **A, Light Box:** yes. This is the "Find us" walk the concept describes, though it starts at the shopfront rather than at Queen Street Mall.
- **B, Ink and Oil:** only as a secondary section. B's hero needs a close broth shot, which this clip does not have.
- **C, Broadside:** yes, as short "window" loops cut from the 4 s, 9 s and 14 s moments.
