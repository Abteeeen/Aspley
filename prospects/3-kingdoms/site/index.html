Two websites for 3 Kingdoms, built from our walk-in footage

Both variants use the same method as the El Pueblo #2 site in the reference video. Scrolling plays one continuous, stabilised take past the red lattice, through the dining room and onto the counter menu, pausing at each landmark. Everything after that is real 3 Kingdoms content: the counter board, the six spice levels, counter prices and how to find Shop 32.

Open Variant B

How the reference site was made

The video shows a creator building the El Pueblo #2 site for a restaurant. These are the steps it shows, which we repeated for 3 Kingdoms.

  1. Film the place

    One clip of the outside, then the left side and the right side of the room. We shot four clips on 13 Oct: the Level A hall, the walk in, and two portrait passes.

  2. Hand it to Claude

    Three zips: the clips and two photo sets. Claude unpacked them, built the site and deployed it to a preview link. Our clips are in the repo, compressed to under 20 MB each.

  3. Scroll becomes the camera

    The clip is cut into frames and drawn on a canvas as you scroll, so the page walks you in. Then the name lands and the rest of the page is ordinary sections with motion.

What is real and what is still to do

Real
  • All walk-in and room imagery is our footage from 13 Oct 2026. The scroll walk-in is one continuous take per device (120702 on desktop, 115928 on phones), stabilised and colour-graded with ffmpeg, scrubbed at 12fps with frame blending and three chapter holds.
  • Dish images are the cards on the counter, cropped from the same footage.
  • The six spice levels come from the counter card: 大骨汤 No spicy, 微辣 Mild, 少辣 Little, 中辣 Medium, 大辣 Extra, 特辣 Extra plus.
  • Prices are the in-store list prices from our research ($20.80 signature, $19.80 mains and noodles).
  • Hours, address, phone and delivery links match Uptown, Google and Uber Eats.
To do before launch
  • Diners and staff appear in the walk-in and the photos: blur faces or get the venue's OK before launch.
  • For the smoothest possible walk-in, reshoot one slow take on a gimbal (see the plan in research/clips/edited/README.md).
  • A proper food shoot. The counter cards are photos of photos behind acrylic.
  • Order pickup is not wired up yet. The buttons jump to the menu.
  • Owner to confirm the Chinese spice names and dish names, and in-store prices.
  • Directions within Uptown need a walk-through to confirm the route from Queen Street.
  • Full audit: web-design-guidelines and react-best-practices passes once one variant is chosen.
