冒菜Chengdu's one-bowl hotpot

Pick your meat, greens and noodles from the ice counter. The kitchen cooks the lot in a broth made fresh each day and sends it out with rice.

Mao cai is the Chengdu dish. Chengdu was the capital of Shu in the Three Kingdoms period, which is where the name comes from.
